Account & Execution

Carry & Margin

Interest accrues on cash every calendar day (ACT/360); borrowed cash pays the cash rate plus the spread, shorts pay the borrow fee on their value. Below the maintenance margin, positions are cut at the close. A rate series (e.g. FRED’s DTB3 T-bill yield) can be added on the Data tab.

1 bps = 0.01%. Orders placed at a bar’s close fill on the next bar, so your strategy never trades on data it couldn’t have seen.

        Research Tools
        Stress-test your strategy before you trust it.
        
    
        
    
        
      
        Optional Tools
        
      
        Always shown: Parameter Sweep, Monte Carlo Resampling & In-Sample vs Out-of-Sample.
        
    
        

    
        
      
        Parameter Sweep
        Re-run your strategy for every combination of PARAMS values & score each run. Tune on older data, then check the result on data the tuning never saw.
        
      
        
        
        
          
        
            
        
            
        
            
        
            
        
            
        
            
        
          
        
          
        Use start:stop:step or a comma-separated list. Up to 400 runs.
        
          
        
          
        
        
        
        
        Pick a parameter & a list of values, then run the sweep.
        
      
        
    
        

    
        
      
        Monte Carlo Resampling
        Shuffle blocks of your strategy’s daily returns into hundreds of alternative histories. A wide spread means the result depends heavily on the order of good & bad days.
        
      
        
        
        
          
        
            
        
            
        
            
        
          
        
          
        
        
        
        
        Runs your current strategy, then resamples its daily returns.
        
      
        
    
        

    
        
      
        In-Sample vs Out-of-Sample
        Split one continuous backtest at a date & compare the parts. An edge that disappears after the split was probably fitted to the past.
        
      
        
        
        
          
        
            
        
            
        
          
        
          
        
        
        
        
        Runs your current strategy & splits the result.
        
      
        
    
        

    
        
      
        Sub-Period Consistency
        Cut the backtest into equal back-to-back periods & score each one. A real edge shows up in most periods; one lucky stretch carrying the whole result is a warning sign.
        
      
        
        
        
          
        
          
        
        
        
        
        Runs your current strategy & splits it into periods.
        
      
        
    
        

    
        
      
        Cost Sensitivity
        Re-run the backtest at rising trading costs. Strategies that trade often can look great at zero cost & lose money at realistic ones; this shows where the edge runs out.
        
      
        
        
        
          
        
          
        Each level replaces your commission % & slippage; the flat fee per fill is kept. Use a list or start:stop:step.
        
          
        
        
        
        
        Runs your current strategy once per cost level.
        
      
        
    
        

    
        
      
        Random-Timing Test
        Shuffle the days your strategy was invested, keeping how often & how much, & pair them with the real price moves. If random timing scores as well as yours, the entries & exits aren’t adding skill.
        
      
        
        
        
          
        
            
        
            
        
          
        
          
        Scores are Sharpe ratios before costs, so they measure timing alone.
        
          
        
        
        
        
        Runs your current strategy, then shuffles its timing.
        
      
        
    
        

    
        
      
        Instrument Analytics
        Buy-&-hold statistics for each loaded instrument & how their daily returns move together. Low or negative correlation is what makes diversification work.
